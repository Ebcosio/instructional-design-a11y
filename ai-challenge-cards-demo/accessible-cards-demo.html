Accessible Navigation Cards — Live Demonstration

This standalone portfolio demonstration reproduces the accessible navigation cards from the AI Learning Journey project. It is an independent showcase of the remediated interface, not the live course website.

To test the cards, use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links and Enter to activate one. You can also explore the card names and descriptions using a screen reader. All ten cards link to the same explanatory page for demonstration purposes.
